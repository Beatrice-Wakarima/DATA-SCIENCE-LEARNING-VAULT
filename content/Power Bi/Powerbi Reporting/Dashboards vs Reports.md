

**Purpose:** Distinguish dashboards from reports.

- **Dashboards** → Single view, real-time metrics, minimal interactivity.
    
- **Reports** → Multi-page, interactive (slicers, filters), detailed analysis.
    
- Reports feel more like applications than dashboards.
    
- Example: Monthly sales table → filter by category in reports; dashboards show separate visuals.
    

## 🗂 Choosing Between Dashboards and Reports

**Purpose:** Clarify use cases.

- **Dashboards** → Rapid status checks, broad audience, often displayed on monitors.
    
- **Reports** → Deep analysis, PC-based, interactive exploration.
