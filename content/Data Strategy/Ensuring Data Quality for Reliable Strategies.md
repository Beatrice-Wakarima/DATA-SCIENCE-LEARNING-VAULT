### 🎯 Purpose

High‑quality data underpins reliable analytics and business intelligence. It ensures decisions are accurate, actionable, and strategically aligned.

### 🧹 Data Cleansing & Validation

- **Cleansing**: Identify and correct errors, inconsistencies, and duplications.
    
- **Validation**: Ensure incoming data is accurate, complete, and standardized.
    
- Tools: Alteryx, Tamr, Informatica.
    
- Outcome: Prevents bad data from corrupting systems, improves relevance for decision‑making.
    

### 🛡️ Data Governance

- Framework for access, usage, and security.
    
- Ensures compliance with **GDPR** (EU) and **CCPA** (US).
    
- Guided by **DAMA‑DMBOK2** (Data Management Body of Knowledge).
    
- Defines roles, responsibilities, and accountability.
    
- Outcome: Ethical handling of data, trust, and reduced risk.
    

### 🔗 Master Data Management (MDM)

- Integrates data from multiple sources into a **single point of reference**.
    
- Avoids duplication and inaccuracies.
    
- Tools: SAP Master Data Governance.
    
- Outcome: Unified view of data, improved operational efficiency, consistent practices.
    

### 🧩 Bringing It All Together

- **Cleansing + Validation** → Reliable inputs.
    
- **Governance** → Integrity, compliance, trust.
    
- **MDM** → Unified, consistent outputs.
    
- Together, these pillars enable advanced analytics and informed strategic decisions.
