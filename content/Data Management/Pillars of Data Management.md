

### 🎯 Enhancing Reliability

Four pillars strengthen data management and ensure reliability:

- **Master Data Management (MDM)**
    
- **Reference Data Management (RDM)**
    
- **Metadata Management**
    
- **Data Quality**
    

Together, they form the foundation for trustworthy, consistent, and actionable information.

### 👥 Master Data Management

- Focus: core business entities (customers, products, employees, suppliers).
    
- Goal: consistency, accuracy, coherence across systems.
    
- Activities: profiling, consolidation, validation.
    
- Benefits: unified view, enhanced quality, informed decisions, operational efficiency.
    

### 🗂️ Reference Data Management

- Focus: static values used for classification (currency codes, country lists, product categories).
    
- Activities: defining, categorizing, updating reference data.
    
- Ensures uniformity and reliability across departments.
    
- Benefits: consistency, reduced errors, improved interoperability.
    

### 📖 Metadata Management

- Metadata = “data about data.”
    
- Provides context: sources, definitions, formats, relationships.
    
- Activities: schema design, data dictionaries, maintenance processes.
    
- Benefits: improved comprehension, discovery, and interpretation of data.
    

### 🎯 Data Quality

- Measures: validity, integrity, timeliness, consistency, completeness, accuracy.
    
- Processes: profiling, cleansing, validation, monitoring.
    
- Ensures data is free from errors, duplicates, inconsistencies.
    
- Benefits: trustworthy analysis, reliable decision‑making, effective utilization.
