

### 🎯 Three Critical Pillars

- **Data Security** → safeguarding confidentiality, integrity, availability.
    
- **Data Governance** → framework for authority, accountability, and compliance.
    
- **Business Intelligence (BI)** → transforming raw data into actionable insights.
    

### 🔐 Keeping Data Secure

- Protects digital data from unauthorized access, corruption, theft.
    
- Measures: encryption, access controls, firewalls, antivirus.
    
- Privacy practices: anonymization, consent management, GDPR/CCPA compliance.
    
- Challenges: cyber threats, compliance complexity, breaches.
    
- Best practices: security audits, employee training, data classification.
    

### 📜 Elements of Data Governance

- Formalized framework for decision‑making, control, accountability.
    
- Components: policies, stewardship roles, compliance with standards.
    
- Frameworks: DAMA‑DMBOK, EDM Council’s DCAM.
    
- Focus: strategic alignment, treating data as a valuable asset.
    
- Contrast: governance = control/authority; management = operational practices.
    
- Challenges: resistance to change, balancing flexibility vs. control.
    
- Benefits: efficiency, trust, organizational alignment.
    

### 📊 Unlocking Insights

- BI: transforms structured internal data into historical, current, predictive views.
    
- Tools: data warehousing, reporting, dashboards.
    
- Accessible to non‑technical users.
    
- Contrast: **Data Science** → broader scope, structured + unstructured data, ML, big data, predictive modeling.
    
- BI = retrospective, user‑centric.
    
- Data Science = forward‑looking, exploratory, interdisciplinary.
    
- Together: empower strategic decision‑making across the organization.
    

### 🛤️ Next Steps

- Document security protocols and privacy compliance measures.
    
- Map governance roles, policies, and frameworks.
    
- Build BI dashboards aligned with organizational goals.
    
- Integrate BI with data science workflows for predictive insights.
