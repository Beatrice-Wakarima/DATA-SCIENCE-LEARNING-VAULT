

**Purpose:** Suite of apps, services, and connectors for rapid, no-code business solutions.

- **AI-powered development** → prebuilt templates, connectors to hundreds of data sources.
    
- **Cross-device support** → works in browsers, phones, tablets.
    
- **No coding required** → Power Fx formulas, drag-and-drop design.
    

## 🗂 App Types

- **Canvas Apps** → Blank canvas or template, flexible layout, Power Fx logic.
    
- **Model-Driven Apps** → Built on Dataverse, data-first, suited for complex processes.
    
- **Power Pages** → External-facing websites, Dataverse-backed, for outside users.
    

## 🗂 Canvas Apps Module

**Focus of this module:**

- Navigate the **Maker Portal**.
    
- Connect to **Excel data**.
    
- Build a working app with screens, formulas, and connectors.
    

## 🗂 Connectivity

- Connect to **cloud services** (SharePoint, SQL, Dynamics, custom APIs).
    
- Interact with **existing data** across devices.
    
- Share instantly with coworkers on mobile.
    

## 🗂 Use Cases

- **Field Equipment** → Replace clipboards with tablets, analyze parts, order replacements onsite.
    
- **Restaurant Management** → Digital schedules, vacation requests, reminders for shifts.
    

## 🗂 Benefits

- Rapid app building with existing data.
    
- Cloud connectivity.
    
- Instant sharing and collaboration.
    
- Transform manual processes into digital workflows.
