Welcome to my digital garden. I am a data professional specializing in building end-to-end, production-grade data solutions that turn raw information into executive-level insights.

My work bridges the gap between complex data infrastructure and actionable business intelligence, utilizing the **Medallion Architecture** to ensure data quality and scalability.

---

## 🧠 Core Expertise

- **Data Engineering** — Designing robust ETL/ELT pipelines using Python, SQL, and Apache Airflow
- **Cloud & Infrastructure** — Orchestrating containerized environments with Docker and managing relational databases like PostgreSQL
- **Business Intelligence** — Developing high-impact dashboards in Power BI using advanced DAX for financial and operational KPIs
- **Machine Learning** — Deploying automated ML models via FastAPI to drive predictive analytics

- **Solar Energy ROI Analysis** — Utilizing Priority Matrices and GHI potential mapping to benchmark investment opportunities
