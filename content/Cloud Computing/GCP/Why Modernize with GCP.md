

## Digital Transformation

- Integration of digital technology into all areas of a business.
    
- Fundamental change in operations, strategy, and customer value delivery.
    
- More than a tech upgrade → cultural shift requiring experimentation and adaptation.
    

## Necessity of Transformation

- Customers expect seamless, on-demand digital services.
    
- Transformation enables efficiency, responsiveness, and faster innovation.
    
- Driven by ubiquity of digital devices (phones, computers).
    

## Retail Example

- Online sales platform expands market reach.
    
- Digital inventory management streamlines operations and reduces costs.
    
- Real-time stock info improves customer service.
    
- Social media + digital marketing enhance engagement and visibility.
    

## Blockers

- Legacy infrastructure: inflexible, inefficient, insecure.
    
- Example: outdated OS → slower performance, vulnerability to cyberattacks.
    
- Upfront investment and operational costs.
    
- Cultural/organizational change and integration complexity.
    

## Advantages of GCP

- Secure, scalable, regularly updated infrastructure.
    
- Opportunity to leave legacy systems behind.
    
- Increased operational efficiency.
    
- Flexible cloud models tailored to business needs.
    

## Cloud Models

### Public Cloud

- Shared among multiple organizations.
    
- Offered by providers like Google, Amazon, Microsoft.
    

### Private Cloud

- Dedicated to a single organization.
    
- Enhanced control, privacy, and compliance.
    
- Ideal for finance and healthcare sectors.
    

### Hybrid Cloud

- Combines public + private clouds.
    
- Flexibility in data/application deployment.
    
- Balance between security and scalability.
    

### Multi-Cloud

- Uses multiple providers to avoid vendor lock-in.
    
- Leverages best solutions for different tasks.
    
- Provides geographic redundancy.
    
- Complex to maintain → GCP’s **Anthos** simplifies management across environments.
