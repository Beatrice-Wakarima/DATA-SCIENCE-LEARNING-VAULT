

## What is GCP?

- Google Cloud Platform is one of the oldest and largest cloud computing services.
    
- Offers unique advantages in a crowded marketplace.
    
- Built on the same backbone that powers Google Search and YouTube.
    

## Global Infrastructure

- Google owns one of the largest fiber optic networks worldwide.
    
- This infrastructure ensures speed, reliability, and scalability.
    
- Commitment to open-source → cost savings, flexibility, and rapid innovation.
    

## Storage Solutions

- Unified storage with robust security and speed.
    
- Databases for transactions, inventory, customer behavior, and more.
    
- Example: secure photo backups without manual effort.
    

## Compute Power

- Google Compute Engine provides scalable virtual machines.
    
- Spin up VMs, perform processing, shut them down easily.
    
- Pay-as-you-go model → no hardware maintenance or electricity costs.
    

## Innovation Tools

- Machine Learning and AI capabilities integrated into GCP.
    
- Tools designed to foster rapid product launches and innovation.
    
- Businesses can scale up or down organically with demand.
    

## Security Layers

- Same protections as Gmail and Google Drive.
    
- Encryption at rest and in transit.
    
- Global network of data centers ensures privacy and compliance.
    

## Business Impact

- Enables rapid product launches without hardware delays.
    
- Democratizes digital transformation for startups and Fortune 500 companies alike.
    
- Pay-as-you-go model supports organic growth.
    

## GCP Offerings

- Infrastructure, storage, compute, AI/ML, and more.
    
- Designed to support modern businesses across industries.
    
- Used by both small startups and large enterprises.
